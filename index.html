About this archive

David Shemtov spends time each year on the international film festival circuit — Berlinale in February, Cannes in May, Venice and Toronto later in the year, plus a few smaller festivals — and writes brief, opinionated dispatches on the films he sees.

For a long time these capsule reviews lived only in a WhatsApp group of friends. This site collects them.

Each review is short by design but never slight. In a paragraph or two David sketches the plot, registers a critical impression, and ends with a score out of five: a small, useful guide for those of us deciding whether a film is worth our time.

Every review here is David's own writing, reproduced with his permission. Where a film has a non-English title, the English-language title is shown above and the original title beneath.
